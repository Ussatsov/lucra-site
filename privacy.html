Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

The short version

Lucra does not collect, send or sell your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What Lucra stores

Lucra stores what you add to it: your stock items and their photos, purchase and sale details, brands, categories, sizes, business expenses and your settings. All of it is kept in the app's own storage on your iPhone. None of it is sent to me or to anyone else.

What Lucra does not do

  • No account, sign-up or login.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No servers: the app works without sending your data anywhere.
  • No third-party services that receive your data.

Camera and photos

Lucra asks for the camera or your photo library only when you choose to add a photo to an item. The photo is saved inside the app on your device and goes nowhere else.

Exports

When you export a CSV file from the UK Tax tab, the file is created on your device. It only leaves your device if you choose to share or save it somewhere.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, Lucra's data is included in that backup, as with other apps. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and I have no access to them.

Deleting your data

You can delete any item or expense inside the app. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes all of its data from the device.

Support emails

If you email me for support, I receive your email address and whatever you write. I use it only to reply to you.

Children

Lucra is not aimed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new date at the top.
