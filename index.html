Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Lucra works straight away and never asks you to sign up.
Where is my data kept?
On your iPhone only. If you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, Lucra's data is included in that backup like any other app's.
How do I move my data to a new iPhone?
Restore the new iPhone from a backup of the old one, or use Apple's Quick Start transfer. Your stock comes across with everything else.
Is the UK tax information tax advice?
No. Lucra shows general information based on gov.uk to help you keep records. Check gov.uk or speak to an accountant about your own situation.
Can I get my records out?
Yes. The UK Tax tab exports a CSV ledger for any tax year, which opens in any spreadsheet app.
